Customer review

Three signals are checked for each period: at most 3 active days, at least 2 tickets, and any ticket marked open in the source extract. Zero signals means Routine, one means Review, and two or more mean Priority review. These groups are for manual follow-up. They express no churn probability.

How outcomes are assigned

A paid invoice in the immediately following month gives Renewed. A cancellation effective on the first day of the following month gives Cancelled. Pending or missing next-period evidence and conflicts are Unknown. The snapshot includes dates before 1 May 2026. The renewal rate uses only known outcomes.

The demo uses calendar-month billing and a single plan per customer. Usage and support totals count records with dates within that invoice month. Ticket statuses come from the extract and can reflect later updates, so the review groups are retrospective. Relationships in synthetic data illustrate the workflow and do not establish causes or real customer behavior.
